HOTEL BOOKING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - ENGINEERING GUIDE
===================================================

1. PURPOSE
----------

This guide describes how to extend and maintain the application after the 2026
controller refactor. It is the implementation-oriented companion to README.md,
ARCHITECTURE.md, and DATABASE_GUIDE.md.

The application is a Qt 6 desktop system using C++17 and SQLite. It manages
rooms, customers, reservations, maintenance, checkout, invoices, dashboards,
PDF reporting, staff sign-in context, and transactional upsert persistence.

The refactor was structural. Existing validation order, error messages, model
semantics, database schema, and view workflows are intended to remain stable.

Time-based operations are now authoritative. Planned booking timestamps use hourly
slots; actual check-in and checkout timestamps use seconds; invoices round actual
duration half-up to billable hours with a one-hour minimum. A completed checkout
creates a two-hour Cleaning block. Confirmed Maintenance and Cleaning reduce
saleable report capacity, while an awaiting Maintenance case is a soft scheduling
hold only. Do not reintroduce nightly calculations or a separate no-show state:
a missed arrival is a cancelled booking with an explanatory reason.


2. PROJECT MAP
--------------

src/models/
    Domain entities: Room hierarchy, Customer, Booking, Invoice,
    RoomMaintenance, MaintenanceGuestNotice, and StaffSession.

src/controllers/hotel/
    HotelManager: stable application facade and cross-domain coordinator.

src/controllers/room/
    RoomManager: canonical owner of rooms, maintenance, and notices.

src/controllers/customer/
    CustomerManager: canonical owner of customers.

src/controllers/booking/
    BookingManager: canonical owner of bookings and invoices; booking lifecycle,
    availability, and billing rules.

src/controllers/report/
    ReportService and report DTOs. Read-only consumer of HotelManager.

src/database/
    DataManager singleton: SQLite schema, migration, staged load, revision
    checking, transactional upsert persistence, rollback, and recovery.

src/views/
    Qt widgets, dialogs, navigation, dashboard, Statistics visualization,
    invoice exploration, PDF rendering, and .ui files.

src/resources/
    Small application icons plus Standard, Deluxe, and Suite room-photo assets.
    CMake embeds the photos through Qt BIG_RESOURCES rather than normal AUTORCC.

There are no controller service directories or forwarding service classes.


3. STARTUP AND LIFETIME
-----------------------

1. main.cpp configures QApplication and resolves data/hotel_data.db.
2. One HotelManager is created on the stack.
3. DataManager::loadAll stages and validates the database snapshot.
4. Staff authentication establishes the StaffSession context.
5. MainWindow and all operational pages receive the same HotelManager pointer.
6. Views issue business commands through HotelManager and commit successful
   mutations through DataManager.
7. The final snapshot is saved when the event loop exits.

Do not construct a second HotelManager for a page, dialog, or shortcut. It would
create a second object graph and break the single canonical state assumption.


4. CONTROLLER ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------

Views / ReportService / DataManager
                  |
                  v
             HotelManager
             /    |     \
            v     v      v
     RoomManager CustomerManager BookingManager

HotelManager owns all three managers persistently by value. Managers do not own,
reference, include, or call HotelManager. No manager stores a reference to another
manager. This keeps dependency direction one-way and makes staged move-assignment
safe.

4.1 Canonical ownership

RoomManager owns:
- vector<shared_ptr<Room>>
- vector<RoomMaintenance>
- vector<MaintenanceGuestNotice>

CustomerManager owns:
- vector<shared_ptr<Customer>>

BookingManager owns:
- vector<shared_ptr<Booking>>
- vector<shared_ptr<Invoice>>

HotelManager owns no duplicate collections. Booking weak pointers must resolve to
the exact RoomManager/CustomerManager objects. Invoice weak pointers must resolve
to the exact BookingManager booking.

4.2 HotelManager

HotelManager is both:
- the stable facade used by UI, reporting, persistence, and main.cpp; and
- the coordinator for workflows spanning multiple domains.

Forward directly for a single-domain operation. Coordinate in HotelManager when
a decision needs data from two or more managers. Never reintroduce private Core
callbacks, service friends, temporary Manager(*this) objects, or manager accessors
for views.

4.3 RoomManager

RoomManager owns and implements room-local behavior:
- registration, subtype creation, validation, and lookup;
- base permanent availability;
- archive, restore, and local deletion;
- maintenance date validation and maintenance-to-maintenance overlap;
- maintenance/notice storage and restoration.

RoomManager must not inspect bookings. HotelManager supplies affected booking IDs
when scheduling maintenance and checks unresolved booking conflicts before
confirmation.

4.4 CustomerManager

CustomerManager owns and implements customer-local behavior:
- international identity normalization and validation;
- phone/name/identity collision order;
- registration, update, booking-customer resolution, lookup;
- archive, restore, local deletion, and database restoration.

CustomerManager must not inspect booking history. HotelManager performs history
and unfinished-booking checks before destructive or archival commands.

4.5 BookingManager

BookingManager owns booking and invoice behavior:
- booking creation/update from canonical Customer and Room pointers;
- lifecycle state, check-in, checkout, and cancellation (including a missed-arrival reason);
- arrivals, departures, status filters, and history queries;
- room overlap and excluded-booking checks;
- date-range availability using rooms/maintenance passed per call;
- invoice validation, snapshots, calculations, lookup, IDs, and restoration.

BookingManager may consume read-only collections supplied as call parameters. It
must not store RoomManager or CustomerManager references.

4.6 ReportService

ReportService stores const HotelManager*. It may call only read APIs. It builds a
detached DashboardReportData value and must not mutate managers or models.


5. CROSS-DOMAIN WORKFLOWS
-------------------------

5.1 Booking creation/update

HotelManager resolves the canonical customer and room, then calls BookingManager
with those pointers and RoomManager's maintenance values. BookingManager preserves
the validation sequence for dates, customer state, room state, capacity,
maintenance, and booking overlap. Updates pass the current booking ID as an
exclusion.

5.2 Availability

BookingManager is the only date-range availability authority. ReservationDialog
and RoomStatusPageWidget call HotelManager::getAvailableRoomsForPeriod().

The view sequence is RoomStatusPageWidget -> RoomInfoDialog -> MainWindow ->
ReservationDialog. A card click never creates or edits a booking. It opens the
read-only room review only after the selected timestamp/capacity remains valid.
RoomInfoDialog accepts only through Booking; its Cancel/close path must not emit
bookingRequested. ReservationDialog performs the final authoritative validation.

SchedulePickerDialog is the shared timestamp-entry component. In Check-in mode,
each date/hour click replaces the arrival and resets provisional checkout to one
hour later. It never retains a stale checkout to infer a range. Check-out becomes
editable only after the user selects Check-out mode. The calendar hides week
numbers and the hour surface is a centered 6 x 4 grid containing all 24 slots.

Overlap uses half-open intervals:

    requestedCheckIn < existingCheckOut
    && existingCheckIn < requestedCheckOut

Cancelled, deleted, and completed records do not block. An active stay
blocks a request covering today until checkout. Maintenance uses the same interval
semantics and only blocks its associated room.

5.3 Maintenance

HotelManager discovers unfinished overlapping bookings. RoomManager stores an
immediately Confirmed case when none exist, or an Awaiting guest response case and
notice records when conflicts exist. Confirmation rechecks live booking overlap
before RoomManager changes statuses.

5.4 Archive and delete

RoomManager and CustomerManager implement local mutation. HotelManager first
checks BookingManager data for unfinished or historical references and preserves
the existing rejection messages.

5.5 Checkout and invoice

Checkout changes booking lifecycle state. Invoice creation remains a separate
command. BookingManager enforces completed state, uniqueness, valid issue/payment
timestamps, locked hourly rate/tax, actual elapsed seconds, rounded billable hours,
payment limits, and immutable customer, room, and stay snapshots.


6. MODEL RULES
--------------

6.1 Room hierarchy

Room is polymorphic. StandardRoom, DeluxeRoom, and SuiteRoom provide subtype
metadata, capacity, and fee behavior. RoomFactory is the construction boundary.
Persistence uses dynamic_pointer_cast only when restoring subtype-specific fees.

6.2 Customer

Customer stores an internal normalized identity key plus document type, issuing
country, document number, legal name, phone, and archive state. Dialog validation
improves feedback; CustomerManager remains authoritative.

6.3 Booking

Booking persists planned dates, actual dates, checked-in/out facts, cancellation
facts, occupancy, locked pricing/tax, and audit timestamps. BookingState is derived:

1. cancelled -> CANCELLED (the reason may explain a missed arrival)
2. checked out -> COMPLETED
3. checked in -> ACTIVE
4. otherwise -> UPCOMING

6.4 Invoice

Invoice belongs to one completed booking. It retains a canonical weak booking link
and immutable billing snapshots so later room/customer edits cannot alter history.
Invoice deletion is rejected; future reversal should use credit-note semantics.


7. PERSISTENCE
--------------

DataManager is the only SQL gateway. Views and managers do not issue SQL.

7.1 Load

Load order is fixed:

    Customer -> Room -> Booking -> RoomMaintenance
             -> MaintenanceGuestNotice -> Invoice

DataManager restores into a temporary HotelManager. Every row must validate. Before
publication, it checks canonical Booking->Customer/Room, Invoice->Booking, and
maintenance/notice foreign references. Only then does it run:

    manager = std::move(loadedManager);

Compile-time assertions require HotelManager move construction and assignment.
Invalid input leaves live state unchanged.

7.2 Save and recovery

saveAll writes the canonical records with INSERT-or-UPDATE statements and deletes
only records that are absent from their canonical collections, all inside one
SQLite transaction. DataVersion prevents a stale process from overwriting a newer
revision. Any SQL failure rolls back. commitChanges restores the last committed
database state if saving fails.

Keep schema, migration, load order, save order, revision handling, and rollback
behavior synchronized whenever persistence changes.


8. VIEW BOUNDARY
----------------

Every operational view stores or receives HotelManager, never a domain manager.
Do not include RoomManager.h, CustomerManager.h, or BookingManager.h from views.
Do not expose hotelManager.roomManager()-style mutable accessors.

StatisticsPageWidget is read-only. It obtains invoice and booking collections from
HotelManager, constructs chart series and invoice filters locally, and opens
InvoiceDialog for details. Keep business mutations in HotelManager commands and
refresh Statistics after booking or checkout changes that affect its source data.

DialogWindowBehavior.h is a view-only presentation helper. It centralizes three
contracts for custom dialogs:
- fixed geometry bounded by the owner window or available screen;
- dragging through an explicit custom header; and
- suppression of mouse-wheel value changes for non-monetary combo/spin inputs.

Price and fee widgets that intentionally support wheel adjustment set the
allowMouseWheelValueChange property before disableNonMoneyWheelChanges() runs.
Do not mark dates, times, occupancy, room measurements, lifecycle selectors, or
identity selectors as monetary. Custom dialog surfaces remain opaque, use the
light-blue perimeter around the entire title/content surface, and keep header
corner styling consistent.

RoomImageCarousel is the single room-gallery implementation used by Room Info
and Room Management. It owns presentation state only: the selected room type,
current photo index, and display cache. It must not query managers, mutate Room,
or decide availability. Only the selected gallery is decoded; QImageReader
decodes close to the requested size, QPixmapCache reuses composed display crops,
a render signature avoids identical work, and a short resize debounce prevents
continuous rescaling while a window is moving.

Room photos live under src/resources/room_images/<type>/. CMake sorts and embeds
them with qt_add_resources(... BIG_RESOURCES ... OPTIONS -no-compress). Keep the
small operational icons in resources.qrc. Do not move full-size JPEG galleries
back into normal AUTORCC: that would regenerate a very large C++ byte array and
needlessly recompress already-compressed images, increasing configure/build cost.



9. CHANGE CHECKLIST
-------------------

Before changing a controller workflow:

[ ] Identify the canonical collection owner.
[ ] Search every HotelManager and view call site.
[ ] Preserve validation and error-message order.
[ ] Keep cross-domain coordination in HotelManager.
[ ] Do not store cross-manager references.
[ ] Update restoration when the object graph changes.
[ ] Verify canonical pointer identity after load.
[ ] Confirm views and ReportService still use HotelManager only.
[ ] Run Debug and Release builds.
[ ] Run `ctest --test-dir build --output-on-failure` with `BUILD_TESTING=ON`.
[ ] Run git diff --check.
[ ] If room photos changed, verify every Standard/Deluxe/Suite resource path,
    open each gallery once, and confirm the previous/next controls wrap safely.
[ ] Confirm resources.qrc still contains only small application icons.

When adding a field:

[ ] Update the model and validation owner.
[ ] Update SQLite creation/migration.
[ ] Update loadAll and saveAll in dependency-safe order.
[ ] Update immutable snapshots if historical output depends on the field.
[ ] Update reporting and documentation where relevant.


10. BUSINESS TEST SUITE
-----------------------

`tests/BusinessWorkflowTests.cpp` is a headless CTest runner. It covers same-day
and overnight bookings, exact adjacent turnover, Cleaning/early-ready, full-day
Maintenance, early check-in/checkout, 29/30/31-minute half-up rounding, room
changes, self-excluding edits, cancellation, customer reuse/new-customer rollback,
locked base rates after later room/optional-fee edits, and legacy SQLite migration. It creates only a temporary legacy
database and never touches `data/hotel_data.db`.

Run it after configuring CMake with `-DBUILD_TESTING=ON`:

    cmake --build build --target HotelBookingManagementBusinessTests
    ctest --test-dir build --output-on-failure

Do not replace these with widget-driven tests. Keep domain tests deterministic and
headless; test visual layout, focus, tooltips, pagination, and dialog wording by
manual UI checklist.

Manual dialog checks must also cover: all seven weekdays visible without horizontal
scrolling; all 24 hour slots visible without overlap; repeated Check-in selection
not preserving a stale Check-out; Room Info appearing before Reservation; header
dragging; fixed/bounded geometry; rounded header corners; and page scrolling that
does not alter a hovered non-monetary input.


11. PROHIBITED REGRESSIONS
--------------------------

Do not reintroduce:
- Service forwarding classes around managers;
- Manager -> HotelManager callbacks;
- friend access used for controller mutation;
- *Core callback pairs;
- temporary manager construction per facade call;
- duplicated writable collections;
- mutable manager getters for views;
- DataManager ownership of model objects;
- reference members that make HotelManager move-assignment unsafe.


12. FINAL VERIFICATION COMMANDS
-------------------------------

Search for obsolete architecture:

    rg "BookingService|CustomerService|RoomService|InvoiceService|RoomAvailabilityService" src CMakeLists.txt
    rg "HotelManager" src/controllers/booking src/controllers/customer src/controllers/room
    rg "friend class .*Service|[A-Za-z]+Core\\(" src/controllers
    rg "room_images/" src/resources/resources.qrc

The last command should return no room-photo entries. Room photo resources are
declared by CMake BIG_RESOURCES; resources.qrc remains the small-icon bundle.

Expected controller tree:

    hotel/HotelManager.*
    room/RoomManager.*
    customer/CustomerManager.*
    booking/BookingManager.*
    report/ReportService.*

The final acceptance standard is unchanged behavior with explicit ownership,
one-way dependencies, safe staged persistence, and HotelManager as the single
application boundary.
